A password is one factor. People can add a second at **Account → Two-step verification**, and a tenant can require one of every member.

## Factors

| Factor | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Authenticator app | Any app that makes 6-digit codes (TOTP). Its secret is encrypted under the master key and never passes through the console. |
| Passkey | A key kept by the device or a password manager, checked with the device's lock. Signs in on its own as well. |
| Recovery codes | Ten one-time codes, made with the first factor. Each works once; make a fresh set when running low. |

Factors are added only on Hayat's own pages, never in a service.

## Signing in with one

After a password, a reset link or Google/GitHub, Hayat asks for the code, the passkey or a recovery code before it starts the session. A
passkey also signs in on its own: **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page counts as both factors, since the device checks its
owner.

Single sign-on respects it too: a session made before someone added a factor, or before their tenant required one, isn't accepted where
the account now needs more.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Passkeys belong to the issuer's host">
  A passkey is bound to the host name in `HAYAT_ISSUER`. It keeps working as long as that host stays the same; moving Hayat to another
  host means everyone adds their passkeys again. An IP address can't hold passkeys.
</Callout>

## Requiring it for a tenant

A tenant's owner or Tenant admin turns on **Require two-step verification** in the tenant's settings. A member who hasn't set it up is
asked to before they get in, on Hayat's own page, right after their password.

People who sign in through the tenant's own [company provider](/docs/sign-in-methods#a-tenants-own-provider) follow that provider's
rules instead.

## Lost every device

An instance admin can reset someone's two-step verification from their page under **Users**. It removes every authenticator app, passkey
and recovery code, signs the person out everywhere, and emails them. They sign in with their password and set it up again.

## Emails people get

Hayat tells the account's owner when an authenticator app or a passkey is added, when a factor is removed, when a recovery code is used,
and when an admin resets their two-step verification. See [Email](/docs/email).
